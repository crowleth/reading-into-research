### Disclaimer, Data Disclosure, & Project Authority

> **Disclaimer:** *Reading into Research* is an independent, student-created navigation guide and outline. **Using this guide does not guarantee placement, hiring, or acceptance into any laboratory, research group, or academic program.** Acceptance into campus research remains solely at the discretion of individual Principal Investigators (PIs), faculty members, lab managers, and official department admissions committees.

> **Evening the Playing Field:** Access to undergraduate research should not depend on existing social capital, insider knowledge of academic jargon, or unwritten rules. By centralizing department directories, demystifying course mechanisms, and providing transparent outreach frameworks, this guide works to lower barrier-to-entry friction and make research opportunities equitable for all Berkeley students.

> **Data Privacy & Zero Tracking:** This site is designed as a static, direct-access resource. **We do not collect, store, track, or sell any personal user data, cookies, or search queries.** Submitting feedback via the Google Form is completely optional and collects no personal information by default unless you choose to provide a contact email. The guide is deliberately built as a streamlined content outline without ads, pop-ups, algorithms, or paywalls. It aims to reduce cognitive overload and prevent burnout by giving you the shortest, clearest route to authoritative campus resources.

> **Ownership & Authority:** This digital guide is authored and maintained independently as a student project by Ethan Crowley. It is not an official publication of the University of California, Berkeley, nor does it carry administrative authority over departmental policy, URAP selection, or official course enrollment. For official university policies, deadlines, and requirements, always cross-reference official departmental websites.
