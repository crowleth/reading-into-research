# Reading into Research 
> **A Student Guide to Navigating How to Get Into Research at UC Berkeley**

[![GitHub Pages](https://img.shields.io/badge/site-live-brightgreen)](https://your-username.github.io/repository-name/)

*Reading into Research* is a digital resource outline and decision guide built to help UC Berkeley undergraduate students navigate, evaluate, and apply to research opportunities without getting lost in institutional jargon or administrative barriers.

---

## How the Guide is Structured

The site is organized into three targeted pathways based on student intent:

* [**Route 1: Foundational Guidance**](./route1.md) — Explains structured programs (URAP, OURS, SURF), independent study units (199/99 courses), and volunteer positions.
* [**Route 2: Open Opportunities Directory**](./route2.md) — Direct access to department directories, active research portals, application windows, and prerequisites.
* [**Route 3: Faculty Outreach & Cold Emailing**](./route3.md) — Step-by-step outreach framework featuring literature grounding tips, in-person office hour strategies, customizable email templates, and follow-up protocols.
