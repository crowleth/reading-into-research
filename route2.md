## Departmental Email Lists & Hidden Markets

Not all open positions are posted on formal websites. To access the "unlisted" research market:

1. **Subscribe to Department Newsletters:** Major departments (e.g., Physics, MCB, Economics, History) send weekly undergraduate digests that frequently feature callouts from graduate students seeking research assistants.
2. **Check Student Association Boards:** Student-run organizations (like the Physics Undergraduate Society, Association of Women in EE/CS, or BioUS) often host research panels and pass around internal lab openings.
3. **Review Lab Websites Directly:** Look at the faculty directory on your department website. PIs sometimes post open positions on their personal lab homepages under tabs labeled "Join Us" or "Openings."
